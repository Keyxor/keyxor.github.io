The other half of the loop. For each box: what the chain leaves behind in
telemetry, the hunting queries and analytics rules built on those artifacts, and
the code-level remediation with severity scoring.

Detection content here is written against observable behaviour rather than
signatures, so most of it generalises past the box that prompted it. Anything
untested says so.
